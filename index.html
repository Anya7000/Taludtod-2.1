Taludtod

Write Filipino verse in forms that keep their shape. Give the AI an idea, or fill in the lines yourself.

Your idea

The AI fills the boxes on the right using the form and metaphor you chose. It is free and shared, so it may pause when busy. Your idea is sent to Google Gemini to write the poem. Writing by hand always works.

Form

Each letter is one line; lines with the same letter must rhyme. The pattern repeats in every stanza. The traditional forms use monorhyme.
